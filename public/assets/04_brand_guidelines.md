# BizEasy - Brand Guidelines

## Brand color principle

BizEasy is not a blue website.

BizEasy is a neutral visual environment in which blue acts as a recognizable brand signal and tangerine acts as a controlled counterpoint.

## Core colors

### BizEasy Blue
`#4155E5`
Primary identity and primary actions.

### Action Blue
`#3995FB`
Hover/emphasis state. Do not use as a second competing brand color.

### Deep Ink
`#111522`
Primary text and high-contrast product surfaces.

### Cloud
`#F5F6F8`
Primary light-page environment.

### Tangerine
`#F47745`
Counterpoint for selected moments of energy, movement, or emphasis.

## Semantic colors

Success `#16865F`
Warning `#D59620`
Error `#D84A4A`
Info `#4187E8`

Never use semantic colors simply because they “look good”. They communicate state.

## Color behavior

Preferred:
- neutral background + dark type + blue CTA
- dark product UI + blue signal + small tangerine moment
- white surface + blue identity + restrained orange emphasis

Avoid:
- blue gradients everywhere
- orange gradients
- rainbow interfaces
- green branded CTA
- tangerine used for every heading
- changing accent color between sections

## Brand proportion

Starting art-direction ratio:
70% neutral
15% dark/ink
10% blue
4% tangerine
1% incidental highlight

Adjust by composition; do not treat ratios as a mechanical requirement.

## Typography

Modern sans-serif first.
No decorative serif introduced only to create “premium” feeling.
Emphasis should normally come from weight, scale, italic or color within the same type family.

## Photography

Show actual business context:
- sellers
- orders
- packaging
- products
- mobile conversations
- customers

Avoid generic startup-office photography.

## Motion

The brand's motion language is directional and transformational:
conversation -> order -> payment -> business workflow.

Motion should feel confident and physical, not noisy.

## Logo

Use supplied BizEasy logo assets.
Do not redraw.
Do not place it over low-contrast photography.
Do not stretch, recolor arbitrarily, or apply effects that alter recognition.
