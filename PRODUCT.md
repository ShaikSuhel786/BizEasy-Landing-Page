# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Small business owners selling on WhatsApp (e.g., bakeries, boutiques, local merchants). They are busy, practical, and need solutions that reduce friction in their day-to-day operations. 

## Product Purpose
To educate users about how BizEasy automates WhatsApp commerce and to drive sign-ups and free trials. It exists to turn unstructured conversations into verified, manageable orders.

## Brand Personality
Modern, Dynamic, Friendly. The brand feels approachable but highly capable, balancing approachability with high-tech automation.
**References**: Apple-like simplicity combined with UMANO-style dynamic, animated layouts.

## Anti-references
Keep it clean. Avoid cluttered, overly complex, or generic SaaS layouts. 

## Design Principles
- **Show, don't tell**: Emphasize interactive, visual demonstrations of the product (like the phone morphing animation) over long paragraphs of text.
- **Frictionless flow**: The page should feel as seamless and easy as the product itself.
- **Expert confidence**: The design should inspire trust, making the user feel their business operations are in safe hands.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA compliance (Standard). Ensure high contrast for text readability, clear focus states, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for heavy animations.
